**EndlessGlory**: An infinite roguelike arena shooter where each run ascends through chaotic, procedurally generated battlefields, chasing ever-greater glory and scores.

- Design a core combat loop with a fluid dodge-and-shoot mechanic that feels responsive and satisfying within the first 15 minutes of play.
- Build procedural level generation that scales in difficulty and complexity across endless floors, introducing new enemy types and environmental hazards every 10 floors.
- Implement a persistent progression system using runic upgrades and unlockable weapon classes that persist across all runs.
- Integrate a local and online leaderboard system to foster replayability and competitive bragging rights.
- Polish the game with a striking neon-meets-ancient art style, dynamic camera shake, and a synthwave score that intensifies as players climb higher.

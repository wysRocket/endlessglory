exceptions are the three.js water normal maps (MIT), the guide webfonts

packs that every player model and several skeleton enemies are built from. The

(callum@levystreet.com)** and are used under the CraftPix premium royalty-free
license. Attribution is provided as a courtesy.

## Unresolved provenance

These ship today but their attribution could not be established from anything in
this repository. They are listed here rather than given a plausible-looking
license row, because a confident wrong attribution is worse than a recorded gap.
Each entry states the strongest evidence available and what would settle it.
`tests/credits_coverage.test.ts` requires every shipped model to appear either in
the tables above or in this section, so nothing can go missing again silently.

| Asset | What the evidence shows | What would settle it |
|---|---|---|
| `creatures/wolf_basic.glb`, `creatures/greyjaw.glb` | Both are the same donor quadruped rig: an identical 51-node skeleton and an identical 14-clip set (`Bark`, `Howl`, `Idle Alert`, `Sneak`, `Sit`, `Fall`) that appears in no other model here and matches no Quaternius pack. Source comments in `src/render/characters/manifest.ts` call this a "Dog_Animation donor skeleton", but that string appears nowhere else in the repo: no file, no spec entry, no bake script. The models carry no Tripo identifiers, unlike every other model credited to that pipeline. The genuine Quaternius wolf was a separate model file, deleted in `fb6a3e506`, so the word "wolf" in the Quaternius row above pointed at nothing shipped while these two rendered every wolf in the game. | The author of `da24f4d83` confirming what the Dog_Animation donor was and under what license. |
| `creatures/wild_boar.glb` | Added as a Sketchfab glTF export: the original commit `72f4bc9a0` contains the wrapper nodes `Sketchfab_model` and `fb000749137341de99bac236bf0169ef.fbx`. The shipped file still carries the material name `anim_Swinecone_01_PM_mat_Swinecone`. 21 of its 32 clips were retargeted from Quaternius rigs. No uploader or license was recorded. | Resolving Sketchfab model `fb000749137341de99bac236bf0169ef` to its uploader and license. |
| `chars/players/Mech/characters/CombatMech.glb` and the repaints in `chars/players/Mech/textures/` | The committed sibling `CombatMech.fbx` embeds the artist's own export path, identifying it as KayKit Patreon "Mystery Characters" Series 5, July 2024. That is a different distribution channel from the CC0 KayKit GitHub packs credited above, so those rows do not cover it. The chroma textures are project repaints derived from its base atlas. | The KayKit Patreon tier terms under which it was obtained. |
| `chars/enemies/skeleton_dagger.glb` | Appears in neither `scripts/assets/specs/characters.json` nor `skeletons_v2.json`, so which KayKit skeleton pack it came from is not recorded. | Confirming which pack and version it was built from. |
| KayKit Adventurers 2.0, the KayKit Paladin pack, and KayKit Skeletons 1.1 | Commit `2133845ed` states outright that these are "the paid KayKit Adventurers 2.0 + Character Animations 1.1 packs". Every shipped player model and four skeleton enemies are built from them. The repo records no license text or purchase account for these, unlike the CraftPix packs above. | The purchasing account and the pack license terms, recorded here the way the CraftPix row does it. |

## Brand marks

The four streamer-platform marks inlined as SVG paths in `src/ui/ui_icons.ts`
(Twitch, X, Kick, YouTube) are the trademarks of their respective platforms,
reproduced monochrome and unmodified in shape solely to identify a link that
points at that platform. They are not project assets, and no endorsement or
affiliation is implied. The Discord (Clyde) mark in the same file is used the
same way.

The Solana and USDC logos shipped as `public/claudium/icons/solana-icon.webp`
and `public/claudium/icons/usdc-icon.webp` are trademarks of their respective
owners, reproduced unmodified solely to identify the corresponding payment
rail in the Claudium buy window (`src/ui/claudium_window.ts`). They are not
project assets, and no endorsement or affiliation is implied.
